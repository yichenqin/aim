Numbered shot combinations

Each shot joins the cue ball to a target, then the target to a pocket. Targets are labeled B1, B2, etc.; pockets are P1 through P6.

The angle is between cue-to-target and target-to-pocket directions: 0° is straight ahead, 90° is a right angle, and 180° reverses direction. Angles are calculated from the corrected 2:1 top-down table coordinates, rather than the original photo. Combinations include blocked and impractical shots.

The center-to-center angle uses cue-to-target and target-to-pocket directions. The ghost-ball angle uses cue-to-ghost and target-to-pocket directions. The ghost ball is tangent to the target, on the side opposite the pocket.
