Guide to the results

Original photo

The cue ball is green and other balls are red. Targets are labeled B1, B2, etc.; pockets are P1 through P6. Each shot number identifies a target and pocket combination. Detected positions are estimates.

Top-down view

The playing surface has a 2:1 ratio, with cyan rails and yellow pocket crosses. All balls have the same diameter: short rail length divided by 19.55555. The dashed ghost ball touches the target on the side opposite the allowed pocket position.

Purple points A1–A4 are one ball radius inward from both adjoining corner rails. A5–A6 are one quarter of a ball diameter inward from the middle-pocket rails. Target directions and distances end at these allowed positions.

Select a shot in the table to see its ghost ball, blue cue-to-ghost line, and orange target direction in the top-down view.

Shot table

Only shots with a ghost-ball angle of 90° or less are listed, initially sorted by that angle. Tap a header to sort ascending; tap it again to reverse. Tap a shot number to select its diagrams.

Four rows are visible at a time; scroll inside the table for more. On phones, swipe the table horizontally for the other columns.

Distances use top-down diagram units: long rail = 800 and short rail = 400. Angles use the corrected top-down coordinates. A straight shot is 0°. Combinations may still be blocked or impractical.

View from cue ball

The diagram shows the equal-size ghost and target circles looking along the cue-to-ghost direction.

Blue dashed circle: ghost ball. Red circle: target ball. Purple: shared area.

Formula: Horizontal overlap (%) = 100 × (1 − sin(θ)), where θ is the ghost-ball cut angle in degrees.

Over 90°: this target direction cannot be produced by a direct cue-ball hit.

This is a percentage of the ball diameter, not its area. At 0° the circles coincide; at 90° they touch. Displayed calculation values are rounded.
